How we find +EV bets

We don't predict results. We look for prices.

Price the market

Throughout the day we work out a fair price for each market from the sharpest prices available.

Post the outliers

When a bookmaker offers more than that fair price, we post it with the books offering it, the lowest price still worth taking and a suggested stake.

Grade it at the close

At kick-off we record the closing price. A price that beats the close is the best short-run evidence that it was a good one, because it is much less affected by luck than results are.

What we don't promise

Profit

Beating the closing price is evidence of good prices, not a promise of profit. Results vary and losing runs are normal. At our suggested stakes, over 550 UK picks (about 10 days of picks) a typical worst drop is 14% of a betting bank, and 26% in 1 run in 20. Over 2,000 picks it is 23%, and 39% in 1 run in 20.

Your accounts

Bookmakers often limit accounts that regularly beat the close. Only bet what you can afford to lose.

Private messages

We never message you first, never ask for payment in a private message, and never ask for your bookmaker login. Anyone who does is not us.
