How it works

We don't predict results. We look for prices.

Price the market. Every few minutes we work out a fair price for each market from the sharpest prices available.
Post the outliers. When a bookmaker offers more than that fair price, we post it with the books offering it, the lowest price still worth taking and a suggested stake.
Grade it at the close. At kick-off we record the closing price. A price that beats the close is the best short-run evidence that it was a good one, because it removes luck.

What we don't promise

Beating the closing price is evidence of good prices, not a promise of profit. Results vary and losing runs are normal: at our suggested stakes, a typical worst drop is 13% of a betting bank.

Bookmakers often limit accounts that regularly beat the close. Only bet what you can afford to lose.

We never message you first, never ask for payment in a private message, and never ask for your bookmaker login. Anyone who does is not us.
